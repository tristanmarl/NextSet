NextSet 0.4.0 is a big update to how the watch looks and what it supports. Every screen was rebuilt around one idea: in the gym you glance at your wrist for a second, so each screen shows only what you need right then.

## New screens

- **Same layout everywhere.** The exercise sits at the top, set dots below it, weight and reps as big numbers in the middle, and heart rate at the bottom. Your eye always knows where to look.
- **Readable on small watches.** Long exercise names wrap onto two lines instead of being cut off ("Seat…" on a fenix 7S is gone).
- **Less text.** Button help, capital-letter labels and "all is well" messages are gone. The icons at the screen edge show what each button does, a ✓ says something worked, and words only appear when something is wrong.
- **Overview, history, editors and Home** were trimmed the same way. History shows one workout per page.

## Rest you can read from the rack

- A **blue ring** runs around the screen with the time left. It turns green in overtime and counts up.
- The centre already shows the **next set's weight and reps**, so you can load the bar or move the pin while you rest.
- **Vibrations told apart by length:** a short tick when a set is logged, a medium buzz 10 s before the rest ends, a long one when it is over.

## Heart rate

- **Live heart rate** on the set and rest screens.
- The **summary** shows the workout's average and maximum.

## Pounds

- Liftosaur accounts in **lb** now work. Weights show and upload in pounds, with 1 / 2.5 / 5 / 10 lb steps. Kilogram accounts are unchanged.

## Programs that ask for values

- **RPE:** sets that log an RPE ask for it right after the set.
- **Prompted variables** (Liftoscript `state.name+`): asked after the exercise's last set.
- Both start at the program's own suggestion. START saves, BACK keeps the suggestion, so a skipped question never blocks the upload.
- **One-sided timed sets** now sync. The watch records the left side, then the right side, and uploads both.

## Simpler finish

- **Finish workout** opens a short review (time, sets, exercises). START saves, BACK asks *Keep working* or *Discard*.
- Discarding a workout, deleting a set and clearing the upload queue only respond to the buttons, never to a tap or a wet screen.

## Fixes

- The superset switch cue is now two single buzzes. The Forerunner 965 ignored the old multi-part vibration pattern.

## Install

Install or update NextSet from the **Connect IQ Store** (search for "NextSet"). Your workouts and settings are kept.

## Not yet confirmed on a real watch

These follow Liftosaur's API documentation and were checked in the simulator and by unit tests, but have not been tried with a real watch or a real upload yet: heart rate on screen, the new vibration lengths, pounds, RPE, prompted variables and one-sided timed sets. Reports are very welcome; see [docs/DEVICE_TESTS.md](https://github.com/tristanmarl/NextSet/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_TESTS.md) and open an issue with your watch model.
