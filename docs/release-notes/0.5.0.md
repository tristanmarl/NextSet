NextSet 0.5.0 runs on older five-button watches and syncs many more Liftosaur programs.

- **fēnix 6 Pro, MARQ Gen 1 and more.** NextSet now needs Connect IQ 3.4 instead of 5, which adds the fēnix 6 Pro / 6S Pro / 6X Pro (Sapphire, Solar), Forerunner 945 LTE, Descent Mk2 / Mk2i / Mk2S and the MARQ Gen 1 editions (Adventurer, Athlete, Aviator, Captain, Commander, Driver, Expedition, Golfer). They were tested in the simulator only so far; reports from real watches are welcome. The fēnix 6 / 6S without "Pro", Enduro (Gen 1) and Forerunner 55 have too little app memory.
- **Any week header.** Programs whose weeks are not titled "Week N" now sync: Liftoscript counts any `# …` line as the next week ("# Week 4 - Deload", "# #29 Preparation - Week 1"). 5/3/1 Boring But Big, Juggernaut and Sheiko failed before with "Liftosaur routine data is invalid".
- **Long programs.** Programs with up to 160 days sync (Calgary 16-Week and Bullmastiff hit the old 48-day limit). The watch still loads only the current week, up to eight days.
- **Unsupported days are skipped.** A day with no exercises, or with exercises NextSet cannot run, no longer fails the whole sync. The notice reads "Synced N days, K skipped", and when Liftosaur's next day was skipped the next synced day is offered.
- **EMOM sets.** A set timer with more than one rep is logged as a weight/reps set.
- **Why a sync failed.** Settings → Diagnostics shows the reason of the last rejected or skipped sync ("Last sync: …").

## Install

Install or update NextSet from the **Connect IQ Store** (search for "NextSet"). Your workouts and settings are kept.

---

NextSet is an unofficial companion app, not made or endorsed by Liftosaur or Garmin. It needs Liftosaur Premium for API access.
